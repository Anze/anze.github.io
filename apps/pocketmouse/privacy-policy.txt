Privacy Policy for Pocket Mouse

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Application
PocketMouse, comprising Pocket Mouse - Home for macOS and Pocket Mouse - Companion for iOS (together, the Application), is an application that allows an iPhone or iPad to control a Mac's pointer. All rights to the Application belong to its developer, Sergii Tatarenkov (the Developer).

License Terms
You may use the Application for any private or business purpose, according to the license agreement provided by the application store where the Application was obtained.

User Data
The Application does not store or process user data. The Application does not send user data to anyone else. Pairing information and settings are stored only on the devices on which the Application is installed.

Permissions
On macOS and on iOS, the Application requests Bluetooth access. This permission is required, because Bluetooth is how your iPhone or iPad and your Mac communicate. It is used only to discover and connect to your own paired devices.
On macOS, the Application may also request Accessibility access, in order to perform taps, scrolling, dragging, and gestures on your behalf. This permission is optional: plain pointer movement works without it, and only those additional actions become unavailable if you decline. Accessibility access is used solely to send input events to the system. It is never used to read the contents of your screen, to observe what you type, or to inspect other applications, and nothing obtained through it is stored or transmitted. You may revoke the permission at any time in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Accessibility.

Privacy
The Application does not collect any personal information and does not transfer any personal information to any third party. The Application communicates only between your own devices, over Bluetooth, and makes no network connections.
