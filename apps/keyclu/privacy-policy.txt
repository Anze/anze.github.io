User Data
The Application stores local usage data on your device - specifically, a count of how many times each shortcut is selected by clicking rather than typing. This count stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The database file is stored openly on your device and can be freely read, edited, or deleted by you at any time.

Permissions
The Application requests Accessibility access. This permission is required, because macOS only allows an application to read another application's menu items with it. The Application uses it to read the menu titles and their keyboard shortcuts of the application currently in focus, and to import and export shortcut data. It is never used to observe what you type, to read the contents of your documents or screen, or to control other applications. Nothing read through this permission is transmitted anywhere.
The Application may also request Notification Center access. This permission is optional and is used only to inform you about updates, imports, and exports. Declining it does not affect anything else in the Application.
You may revoke either permission at any time in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

Privacy
The Application does not collect any personal information. All usage data described above stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere - not to the Developer, not to any third party, not to any server.
The Application makes no network connections except to check whether a newer version is available, and, when that is enabled too, to download it. Both the check and the download are optional and off unless you turn them on, the Application works fully without either, and neither sends any personal information or usage data.
