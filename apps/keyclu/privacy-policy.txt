User Data
The Application stores local usage data on your device — specifically, a count of how many times each shortcut is selected by clicking rather than typing. This data is used only to improve the Application's local behavior (for example, prioritizing shortcuts you use most) and is never sent anywhere. The database file is stored openly on your device and can be freely read, edited, or deleted by you at any time.

Privacy
The Application does not collect any personal information. All usage data described above stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere — not to the Developer, not to any third party, not to any server.
