Napkn

per-app sticky notes

macOS 14+ Apple Silicon + Intel Free
Download for macOS

What it does

Hold a modifier. The note for the app you're in appears. Release it. Gone.

A per-app scratch surface, always connected to the work you're doing.

No windows to manage, no notes to file, no context to choose.

Two ways to use it

Write where you work. Your note belongs to the app you're currently using.

Peek

Hold your modifier to see the note for the current app. It stays out of the way and disappears when you release.

Pin

Turn the note into an active scratchpad. Edit freely, and it stays available while you work. Close it with Escape, the close button, or the hotkey.

Pinned notes remain associated with their apps, even while you switch between other apps or restart Napkn.

Features

Built for fleeting thoughts - the things you need right now.

Tied to the app

Every app gets its own note. Switch apps and the note switches with you - no window hunting, no deciding where a note should live.

Hotkey or modifier

Open with a hotkey, or peek by holding a modifier key. When you're done, let go.

Saved as you type

Every keystroke is saved automatically, so a scratch note doesn't disappear just because you moved on.

Out of your way

Napkn stays invisible until you need it. No windows, no filing, no cloud - just the note you need, where you need it.

Hidden when sharing

Excluded from screen sharing by default, so scratch notes stay out of presentations and recordings. Change it by tapping eye if you want them visible.

Private by Design

No accounts, no tracking, no ads, nothing sent anywhere. Your notes are stored locally on your Mac.

What people keep in it

Things that matter for the next ten minutes, not the next ten years.

  • Copy something before you forget it
  • Jot down a command while coding
  • Keep temporary details beside a Finder task
  • Capture an idea without opening a full note app
  • Leave a reminder that belongs to the app you're using

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple Silicon and Intel supported.

Permissions

Pinned notes work straight away. Only peek needs an Input Monitoring permission.

Input Monitoring - optional

Pinned notes work without granting anything. Open, edit, and keep them beside your work from the first launch.

Input Monitoring permission needs so Napkn can tell when you hold the modifier for peek.

Grant it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring.
