You may use the Application for any private or business purpose, according to the license agreement provided by the application store where the Application was purchased.

User Data
The Application stores the notes you write locally on your Mac. The Application does not require an account, does not use cloud sync, and does not send your notes or any other user data to the Developer or to anyone else.

Permissions
The Application may ask for the Input Monitoring permission. This permission is optional. It is used only to detect when you hold the modifier key that shows a note, and it is never used to record, store, or transmit what you type. If you do not grant it, pinned notes continue to work and only the peek behaviour is unavailable. You may revoke the permission at any time in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Input Monitoring.

Network
The Application sends nothing about you anywhere. No analytics, no crash reports, no diagnostics, no user data - not to the Developer, and not to anyone else.

Privacy
The Application does not collect any personal information and does not transfer any personal information to any third party. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no usage tracking, and no advertising. Nothing about you is collected, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.
